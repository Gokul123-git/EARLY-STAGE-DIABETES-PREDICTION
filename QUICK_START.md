# Quick Start Guide - DiabetesAI Platform

**Get up and running in 2 minutes!**

---

## 🚀 For Users

### Step 1: Complete Assessment (30 seconds)

**Option A: Quick Import**
1. Click **"Import Data"** button
2. Connect wearable OR upload CSV/PDF
3. Auto-fills your health metrics
4. Click **"Calculate Risk"**

**Option B: Manual Entry**
1. Enter 7 health metrics:
   - Age
   - BMI (or height/weight)
   - Blood Pressure
   - Fasting Glucose
   - Physical Activity
   - Family History
2. Click **"Calculate Risk"**

### Step 2: View Results (instantly)

Your results show:
- ✅ Risk Level (Low/Moderate/High/Very High)
- 📊 Risk Score (percentage)
- 📈 Interactive charts
- 💡 Personalized recommendations

### Step 3: Get AI Help (anytime)

1. Click the **large purple button** in bottom-right
2. Ask anything about diabetes or your results
3. Get instant, personalized answers
4. Continue conversation naturally - **no refreshing!**

**Example Questions:**
```
"What's my risk level?"
"How can I prevent diabetes?"
"Explain my glucose level"
"What should I eat?"
"Best exercises for me?"
```

### Step 4: Track Progress (optional)

1. Click **"Sign In"** to save assessment
2. View **"Assessment History"**
3. Track risk changes over time

## 🤖 Using the AI Chatbot

### Quick Tips:

✅ **Large Interface** - 700px × 800px on desktop, maximize for full screen  
✅ **Fast Responses** - Answers in ~400ms  
✅ **Continuous Chat** - No refreshing between questions  
✅ **Context-Aware** - Knows your assessment results  
✅ **Always Available** - 24/7 health education  

### Best Questions:

**Understanding Results:**
- "What does my [metric] mean?"
- "Is my risk level serious?"
- "Explain my recommendations"

**Taking Action:**
- "How do I reduce my risk?"
- "What should I do first?"
- "Create a plan for me"

**Learning:**
- "What is prediabetes?"
- "Tell me about Type 2 diabetes"
- "What are the symptoms?"

### Pro Tips:

1. **Be Specific**: "How can I lower my glucose from 105?" vs "Help me"
2. **Follow Up**: "Tell me more about that" continues the topic
3. **Use Context**: "Based on my results..." gets personalized advice
4. **Click Suggestions**: Tap suggested questions for instant answers
