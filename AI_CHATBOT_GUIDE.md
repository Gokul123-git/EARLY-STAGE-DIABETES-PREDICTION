# AI Chatbot Guide

The DiabetesAI Risk Assessment app includes an intelligent AI chatbot that provides personalized guidance, answers questions about diabetes risk, and helps users understand their assessment results.

## 🤖 Features

### Intelligent Conversation
- **Context-Aware**: Knows your assessment results and health metrics
- **Personalized Responses**: Tailored advice based on your risk profile
- **Natural Language**: Ask questions in plain English
- **Comprehensive Knowledge**: Covers diabetes prevention, risk factors, and lifestyle changes

### What the Chatbot Can Help With

#### 1. Assessment Results Explanation
- Understand your risk level (Low, Moderate, High, Very High)
- Interpret your risk score
- Learn what your results mean for your health
- Get action items based on your risk

#### 2. Health Metrics Interpretation
- **BMI**: Understand your body mass index and weight category
- **Blood Glucose**: Learn what your fasting glucose levels mean
- **Blood Pressure**: Interpret your BP readings
- **Physical Activity**: Evaluate if you're meeting recommendations
- **Family History**: Understand genetic risk factors

#### 3. Prevention Strategies
- Evidence-based prevention methods
- Lifestyle modification recommendations
- Diet and exercise guidance
- Weight loss strategies
- Stress management tips

#### 4. Diabetes Education
- What is Type 2 diabetes?
- Prediabetes information
- Warning signs and symptoms
- Testing and diagnosis methods
- Complications and why prevention matters

#### 5. Personalized Recommendations
- Specific actions for your risk level
- Priority interventions
- Timeline for improvements
- Monitoring suggestions

## 💬 How to Use

### Opening the Chat
1. Look for the **large purple/blue gradient button** in the bottom-right corner
2. Click the button with the message icon
3. The chat window will open at a comfortable size

**NEW!** Desktop users can click the **maximize button** for full-screen experience.

### Asking Questions
Simply type your question and press **Enter** or click **Send**. The conversation flows naturally without any refreshing!

**🎯 Conversation Flow:**
```
You: "What's my risk level?"
AI: [Explains your risk]
You: "How can I reduce it?"        ← No refresh needed!
AI: [Provides strategies]
You: "Tell me more about diet"     ← Continues naturally!
AI: [Detailed diet guidance]
```

**Example Questions:**
- "What's my risk level?"
- "How can I prevent diabetes?"
- "What does my BMI mean?"
- "Explain my glucose levels"
- "What exercises should I do?"
- "Tell me about prediabetes"
- "What diet changes should I make?"

### Quick Questions
When you first open the chat, you'll see **suggested questions** based on your assessment. Click any suggestion to ask that question instantly.

### Continuous Conversation
✅ **No page refreshing** between questions  
✅ **Instant responses** (400ms average)  
✅ **Full conversation history** maintained  
✅ **Auto-scroll** to latest message  
✅ **Type and send immediately** - no waiting  

### Window Controls
- **Maximize** (desktop) - Expand to full screen
- **Minimize** - Restore to normal size
- **Reset** (circular arrow) - Start fresh conversation
- **Close** (X) - Close chat window

### Size Options
- **Mobile**: Nearly full screen (95% width)
- **Tablet**: 600px × 750px
- **Desktop**: 700px × 800px
- **Maximized**: Full screen with margins

## ⚠️ Important Limitations

### What the Chatbot IS:
✅ An educational tool  
✅ A source of evidence-based information  
✅ A guide for understanding risk factors  
✅ A helper for lifestyle planning  

### What the Chatbot IS NOT:
❌ A medical professional  
❌ A diagnostic tool  
❌ A prescription service  
❌ A replacement for healthcare visits  
❌ Emergency medical assistance  

### Medical Disclaimer
**Always displayed:** "AI responses are educational only, not medical advice"

**Key Points:**
- Information is general education, not personal medical advice
- Cannot diagnose conditions
- Cannot prescribe medications
- Cannot provide emergency care
- Should not replace consultations with healthcare providers

### When to See a Doctor
The chatbot will recommend seeing a healthcare provider for:
- High or Very High risk levels
- Elevated glucose levels (≥100 mg/dL)
- Diabetes symptoms
- Medication questions
- Diagnosis confirmation
- Treatment planning

### System Prompt Design
For production AI, use a comprehensive system prompt:

```
You are a certified diabetes educator and health coach with expertise in Type 2 diabetes prevention. Your role is to:

1. Provide evidence-based information about diabetes risk factors
2. Explain assessment results in clear, accessible language
3. Offer personalized lifestyle recommendations
4. Answer questions about diet, exercise, and prevention
5. Encourage users to see healthcare providers when appropriate

Guidelines:
- Always be encouraging and supportive
- Use simple, jargon-free language
- Provide specific, actionable advice
- Reference clinical guidelines and studies when relevant
- NEVER diagnose conditions or prescribe medications
- ALWAYS recommend professional medical care for serious concerns
- Respect user privacy and data sensitivity

Context about user:
- Risk Level: {riskLevel}
- Risk Score: {riskScore}%
- Key Metrics: Age {age}, BMI {bmi}, Glucose {glucose} mg/dL
- Recommendations: {recommendations}
```
