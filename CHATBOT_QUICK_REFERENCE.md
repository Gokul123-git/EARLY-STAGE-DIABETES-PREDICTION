# AI Chatbot Quick Reference

## 🎯 Quick Start

**Open Chat**: Click the purple button in bottom-right corner  
**Ask Question**: Type and press Enter  
**Close Chat**: Click X in header  
**Reset**: Click circular arrow icon

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Get Better Answers
✅ **DO**: "I'm 50 with BMI 28 and glucose 105. What should I focus on?"  
❌ **DON'T**: "Help"

✅ **DO**: "How can I lower my glucose from 110 to under 100?"  
❌ **DON'T**: "Fix my sugar"

✅ **DO**: "What exercises work for someone with knee problems?"  
❌ **DON'T**: "Exercise?"

### Use Follow-Up Questions
1. Ask initial question
2. Get response
3. Ask "Tell me more about [specific part]"
4. Request "Give me specific examples"

### Reference Your Data
- "Based on my assessment..."
- "Given my risk level..."
- "With my current metrics..."
- "Looking at my recommendations..."

## 🎨 Response Formats

### The Chatbot Can Provide:

**Numbered Lists**
1. Step-by-step instructions
2. Prioritized recommendations
3. Sequential processes

**Bullet Points**
• Quick tips
• Food lists
• Symptom lists

**Explanations**
Detailed paragraphs about concepts, with bold emphasis on **key points**

**Specific Metrics**
- Your BMI: 27.5 (overweight)
- Your Glucose: 105 mg/dL (prediabetes range)
- Your Risk: Moderate (45%)

## 🚦 What the AI Can/Cannot Do

### ✅ CAN Help With:
- Explaining your assessment results
- Interpreting health metrics
- Providing prevention strategies
- Answering diabetes questions
- Giving diet and exercise guidance
- Explaining medical terms
- Offering motivation and support

### ❌ CANNOT Do:
- Diagnose medical conditions
- Prescribe medications
- Provide emergency care
- Access your medical records
- Replace doctor visits
- Make treatment decisions
- Guarantee health outcomes

## 💬 Tone & Style Guide

### How to Get Best Responses

**Conversational**: ✅ "Can you help me understand my BMI?"  
**Formal**: ⚪ "Please provide information regarding body mass index interpretation."  
**Too casual**: ⚪ "yo what's up with my weight"

**Specific**: ✅ "What exercises work for people over 50?"  
**Vague**: ❌ "Exercise stuff"

**Focused**: ✅ "How do I lower my glucose from 105 to 95?"  
**Scattered**: ❌ "Tell me everything about health"
